This is a personal notebook for technical writing on artificial intelligence. The
goal is depth over volume: each post works through one idea carefully enough to
reimplement.

## Topics

- **Transformers and architectures** — attention, normalization, and positional
  encoding.
- **Training and inference** — optimization, scaling, quantization, and the
  systems that make large models practical.
- **Mathematics for machine learning** — derivations and numerical examples.
- **Papers and evaluation** — what a result claims, and how to reproduce it.
